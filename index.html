Sebenzisa kanjani? How to use it?

Landela la manyathelo: Follow these steps:
INYATHELO 1 STEP 1

Thatha into encinci ngomnwe Take a small amount on your finger

INYATHELO 2 STEP 2

Faka kolu lusu olomileyo Put it on the dry skin

INYATHELO 3 STEP 3

Hlikihla kancinci ngeenxa ezijikelezayo Rub in gently, in circles

INYATHELO 4 STEP 4

Sebenzisa yonke imihla — kusasa nangokuhlwa Use it every day — morning and evening

Vala ijar kakuhle emva kokusebenzisa, ubuyise endaweni epholileyo. Close the jar well after use, and keep it in a cool place.
